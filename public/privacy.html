MART Orders – Privacy

MART, the student-run campus store at MDI Gurgaon. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Who uses this tool

MART Orders is an internal tool for the MART team. Only MART team members on an approved list can sign in, with their @mdi.ac.in Google account. Students do not sign in.

What we store

  • Team members: name, MDI email and role, taken from Google sign-in (name and email only).
  • Students who ordered merchandise: name, roll number, MDI email, phone number, batch, course, section and the items they ordered, imported from the MART order form they filled in.
  • Handovers: when an item was handed over, by which team member, and the name and roll number of anyone who collected it on a student's behalf.
  • A change log of who changed what and when, so mistakes can be traced.

What we use it for

Only to track stock and hand orders to the right students, and to email students when their order is ready to collect. We do not sell or share the data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

In a Supabase database (Mumbai region) that only signed-in MART team members can read. Google sign-in is used only to confirm who a team member is; we do not access anything else in your Google account.

Questions or removal

Write to the MART team at the store, or email georgeoliverjoel@gmail.com, to see or remove your data.
